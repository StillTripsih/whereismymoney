# Privacy Policy for WHERE IS MY MONEY

Effective date: 2026-11-09

WHERE IS MY MONEY (WIMM) is designed to work without an account. The app stores expenses, budgets, categories, savings, language choices, and backups locally in the browser or installed app storage on the device.

## Data we do not collect

The app owner does not operate a server that receives or stores your financial records, backups, or transaction history in the public web version.

## Optional receipt analysis

Receipt analysis is off unless you connect your own Google AI Studio key. When you choose to analyse a receipt, the selected image, audio recording, or text and the category list are transmitted directly from your device to Google Gemini. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's policies. Do not submit information you do not want to send to Google.

Your Google AI Studio key is held only in the current browser session. It is not included in exports or sent to the app owner.

## Device permissions

The app asks for camera or microphone access only after you select a related feature. You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device or browser settings and continue using manual entry.

## Retention and deletion

You control local records. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser/app data, changing devices, or uninstalling the app. To delete local records, use the app's data controls or clear the app/site data in your device settings. The public web version has no central account or database from which the owner can delete your records.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published in this file with an updated effective date. For non-sensitive questions, use the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/StillTripsih/whereismymoney/issues). Do not post private financial information, keys, or receipt images in public issues.
